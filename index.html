Straight answers.

Q.1Do we need to know anything about AI?

No. You know your business; we work out where AI fits and handle the setup.

Q.2What happens to our information?

It's used only to reply to you and to do your assessment, and it's never sold or passed to anyone outside that work. The form on this page is delivered to our inbox by an email relay service; the details are on our privacy page. We'll sign an NDA if you'd like, and we always ask before recording a conversation.

Q.3Do we keep the assessment if we don't hire you?

Yes. The findings and the plan are yours, whatever you decide.

Q.4What does it cost?

The 15-minute consultation is free. The assessment for an owner-led business is $999 and ends with a written plan you own. Putting the fixes in place is a separate step, quoted per job, and you can do it yourself from the plan. For larger companies the in-depth assessment is quoted after a scoping call, and the ongoing work is a monthly retainer.

Q.5How fast do we see something?

The free 15 minutes happen within a few days of your request. The assessment report arrives about a week after the interview. If you hire us for the setup, the first fixes go in within days of the review call and the working demo follows.
